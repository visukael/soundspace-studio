Frequently Asked

How do I book a session?

expand_more
You can book directly through our online portal by clicking the 'Book Session' button. Select your preferred date, time, and studio suite, and our team will confirm your reservation within 24 hours.

Can I bring my own equipment?

expand_more
Absolutely. While we provide a Tier 1 selection of professional analog and digital gear, you are more than welcome to bring your own instruments, specialized microphones, or outboard gear to ensure your signature sound.

Is audio engineer assistance included?

expand_more
Yes, every booking includes a dedicated assistant engineer to help with setup and technical routing. For full production and mixing sessions, our senior engineers are available as part of our Professional and Full Production tiers.

Do you support podcast production?

expand_more
We do. Our Studio B is specifically optimized for high-end podcasting and voice-over work, featuring broadcast-ready signal chains, acoustic treatment for vocal clarity, and multi-mic configurations.

How long does mixing & mastering take?

expand_more
The timeline varies based on project complexity, but typically a single track is delivered within 3-5 business days. We provide up to two rounds of revisions to ensure the final master meets your exact specifications.
